Read this file before writing an edition.

They are not full desk sweeps. The editions of 23–26 October are the fuller filings.

 It is the memory of what has already been filed. An item belongs in the briefing only when today's reading packet changes the last state, or when a next date arrives. Repeating the last state is not news.

Each episode is taken from an edition already in the archive or from the 26 October edition that replaced the earlier filing that morning. Where the later edition did not carry an earlier item, the episode says so. Do not treat a dropped item as disproved. Treat it as not reconfirmed under the source rule.

Confidence on a new item is unchanged. High is three independent sources, including one in the country's own language and one government or embassy page, or five independent sources when that mix cannot be found. Medium is two. Low is one named source from the standing list, and the summary says so.

## Japan MMX and the Idefix rover

Last state, filed 2026-10-26: JAXA's Martian Moons eXploration probe is on a transfer orbit toward Mars. An H3 rocket lifted off from Tanegashima at 19:41 UTC on 19 October, the tenth H3 flight. JAXA confirmed separation about one hour and eleven minutes later. The probe carries Idefix, a rover built by CNES and DLR, and MIRS, a French infrared spectrometer.

What would count as a change: a trajectory correction, a stated arrival date at the moons, a problem with Idefix or MIRS, or a change to the sample-return plan.
