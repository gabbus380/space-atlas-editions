# Change watch

The daily briefing editor appends lines here after each edition. It does not rewrite a line that is already here. During worldwide bootstrap, the country and ecosystem builders may read this file for context, but it does not limit or prioritise away their deterministic 235-entry queues. Only after their completion gates pass do the monthly maintenance agents use this watch as their primary change queue. They do not delete lines. What they did is in their own reports.

A line is:

`- YYYY-MM-DD | observed | ISO3 | country | reason`
`- YYYY-MM-DD | observed | ISO3 | ecosystem | reason`
`- YYYY-MM-DD | expected | ISO3 | country | what arrives, and when`
`- YYYY-MM-DD | expected | ISO3 | ecosystem | what arrives, and when`

`ISO3` is a code in `countries.json`. `country` means the country file. `ecosystem` means the organizations. One line per country per edition. A quiet desk adds nothing.

If the heading for the current UTC month is missing, add it at the end, then append. Do not browse to invent an ISO3. Use the code already on the edition item. If the item has a name and no code, skip it.
